# Relationship Review Bundle

Use this flexible shape for direct proposals. Omit unsupported categories and
fields that do not improve the decision. In embedded mode, carry the required
fields into the invoking workflow's bundle instead of creating another one.

Lead with the relationship conclusion and why it matters now. State the mode,
query time, source coverage as sufficient, partial, or insufficient, and only
the source gaps that limit a conclusion. Distinguish observed fact, inference,
uncertainty, and user-supplied context.

For a safely resolved person, identify the canonical Person note without
exposing private source identifiers. For an unresolved identity, show the
candidate notes and evidence categories that conflict or remain missing; do
not attach the private evidence to either candidate.

## Contextual connections

When discovery is warranted, present the primary connection as:

> **[Person] matters now because [current evidence and concrete reason].**
>
> Useful action: [one plausible action and how it could benefit the current
> work or relationship]
>
> Evidence and limits: [canonical relationship context, current source, and
> material uncertainty]

Optionally append one separate item labeled **Wildcard connection**. Explain
the broader but defensible connection and why it may be worth considering. A
wildcard contains no proposed Task, communication, or Person-note effect unless
the user explicitly promotes it.

## Proposed effects

Number effects continuously within this bundle. Keep every destination effect
independent, even when several follow from the same interaction. One action
affects one authoritative target. When an intended outcome changes multiple
targets, show each target change as a separate numbered action with explicit
dependencies and a separate result.

> **Action 1. [Complete sentence describing one effect.]**
>
> Acting identity: [configured account or identity]
>
> Destination and exact target: [authoritative system plus exact record,
> person note, task location, issue, recipients, or writing backlog]
>
> Proposed content or effect: [complete content or precise change]
>
> Evidence and reason: [native source and time, current destination check, why
> the effect is relationship-load-bearing or useful now]
>
> Coverage and uncertainty: [material source or identity limits]
>
> Depends on: [visible action numbers, or None]

For a Person-note merge, show the survivor update and duplicate trash as
separate actions. The duplicate-trash action depends on a successful readback
of the survivor action, the applicable approved trash-proof action, and any
separate backlink or alias repair actions. Never present "merge these notes" as
one action or one result.

Use these canonical destinations:

- Person notes for approved durable relationship meaning and monotonic contact
  dates, through the Obsidian CLI only.
- The configured task system for a dated relationship follow-up.
- The caller-owned task or issue system for unrelated work.
- The conversation for editable communication text; creating a draft or
  sending is outside this skill.
- The configured writing backlog for an approved writing suggestion.

Report an existing equivalent as **Already satisfied** outside the numbered
novel effects. If no person or effect passes the relevance, identity, evidence,
and duplicate checks, state **No relationship action** and stop without filler.

Invite the user to approve, edit, defer, or skip each action number
independently. State that every approval will be rechecked against identity and
the canonical destination before one application attempt and readback.
