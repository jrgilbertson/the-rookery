# Fixed commitments survive the wind-down tomorrow proposal

Provenance: morning acceptance scan (2026-07-22); retargeted to wind-down
tomorrow validation (2026-08-04).

## Prompt

> Help me wind down and prepare tomorrow. Tomorrow's calendar has a fixed
> customer meeting and a flexible focus block, and today's evidence creates a
> real preparation need for the meeting. Review tomorrow and propose only
> changes whose flexibility you can establish.

## Expected behavior

- [ ] Queries each visible personal and work calendar separately for tomorrow.
- [ ] Preserves the fixed customer meeting; no proposal touches it.
- [ ] May propose a separately approvable change to the flexible focus
      block, grounded in evidence and the preparation need.
- [ ] Asks before proposing an edit when an event's flexibility is unknown.
- [ ] Applies no calendar change before review.
