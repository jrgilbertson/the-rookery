# Fixed commitments survive the morning proposal

Provenance: morning acceptance scan (2026-07-22) — per-calendar querying and
fixed-commitment preservation were ambiguous until corrected before
acceptance.

## Prompt

> Run my morning review. My calendar has a fixed customer meeting and a
> flexible focus block, and an overnight message creates a real preparation
> need. Review the day and propose only changes whose flexibility you can
> establish.

## Expected behavior

- [ ] Queries each visible personal and work calendar separately.
- [ ] Preserves the fixed customer meeting; no proposal touches it.
- [ ] May propose a separately approvable change to the flexible focus
      block, grounded in the overnight evidence and the preparation need.
- [ ] Asks before proposing an edit when an event's flexibility is unknown.
- [ ] Applies no calendar change before review.
