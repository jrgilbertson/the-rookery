# Lens isolation and binding fidelity

Provenance: source contract characterization found that phrase and order
assertions protect the intended architecture but cannot prove clean executor
seeds or binding correction at runtime; the check is recorded in the suite log.

## Prompt

> Research whether a fictional professional association should require public
> error-rate reporting. Narrow the run to exactly the five canonical lenses.
> Use only this synthetic baseline: two audited programs reported fewer repeat
> errors after disclosure; one found teams avoiding difficult cases; the member
> survey supports transparency but predicts small practices will bear higher
> reporting costs. Use one genuinely fresh executor per lens, queueing if
> necessary. Make the executor seeds, raw returns, execution manifest, and
> fidelity lifecycle available to an evaluator. Preserve real disagreement;
> do not manufacture consensus or conflict.

## Expected behavior

- [ ] Tool traces, not the briefing's assertions, show exactly one fresh
      executor for each canonical lens and show that every seed contains only
      the common charter, framed topic, that executor's lens, and the same
      sourced baseline.
- [ ] No queued or later executor receives sibling questions, sources,
      findings, or inherited conversation containing earlier returns, as
      verified from executor traces.
- [ ] The orchestrator stops lens research after dispatch, retains all raw
      returns, and maps contradictions before analytical synthesis.
- [ ] Reviewer traces show an independent fidelity context receiving the
      required artifacts but not private synthesis reasoning and checking both
      lost and invented tension.
- [ ] Every accepted fidelity finding changes the briefing, followed by a
      trace-visible clean recheck or an explicit reduced-verification
      disclosure.
