# Systems-thinking scope and exploration

Provenance: 2026-08-02 approved terminology audit — the prior live contract
called a qualitative cross-lens pass `System dynamics` and the suite lacked a
non-null exploratory case that distinguished systems thinking from a formal
modeling deliverable.

## Prompt

> Produce a full exploratory research briefing explaining fictional
> Northstar's three-quarter sales decline, without an intervention verdict.
> Use only this synthetic source packet: campaign-generated leads rose 30% in
> quarter one; onboarding backlog grew from 10 to 35 days over three quarters;
> refunds rose from 4% to 11%; support staffing stayed flat; Northstar cut
> prices in quarter two; competitors added free migration; hiring is frozen
> for two quarters; and the parent company allocates marketing budget from the
> prior quarter's lead volume. Treat these as sourced facts, make all causal
> uncertainty visible, and use no external sources.

## Expected behavior

- [ ] Lens questions investigate relevant mechanisms, constraints, system
      relationships, and downstream effects before the final synthesis.
- [ ] Integrates supported systems thinking into the relevant findings without
      creating mandatory first-principles or systems-thinking sections.
- [ ] States a useful purpose or function for the focal system without
      presenting an inferred organizational objective as a sourced fact.
- [ ] Explains the observed three-quarter pattern and situates Northstar within
      the relevant customer, competitor, staffing, and parent-budget systems.
- [ ] Traces direct and higher-order effects link by link, preserving evidence,
      inference labels, and calibrated confidence.
- [ ] Names backlog accumulation, capacity, or rate limits only where they
      materially explain the evidence and does not require a causal-loop
      diagram, stock-and-flow model, equation, or simulation.
- [ ] Preserves an exploratory ending with a frontier question rather than
      forcing a verdict or intervention plan.
- [ ] Reports an honest null for any unsupported feedback, delay, nonlinearity,
      path dependence, emergence, stock/flow detail, spillover, or higher-order
      relationship when the absence matters, instead of filling a checklist.
