# Synthetic sources: public error-rate reporting

These sources are fictional test data. Treat each record as an independently
inspectable permitted source, not as a claim from the shared baseline.

## Audited program results

Source type: primary audit summaries from two programs.

Both programs reported fewer repeat errors during the year after public
disclosure. Neither audit used a control group.

## Case-selection study

Source type: primary observational study.

The study found that teams subject to disclosure accepted fewer difficult
cases. It could not determine whether reporting caused the change.

## Member survey

Source type: primary survey.

Most respondents supported transparency. Small practices expected higher
reporting costs and were more likely to predict that difficult cases would be
referred elsewhere.
